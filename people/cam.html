Postdoctoral Fellow

Cam

Cam is a post-doctoral fellow in the Xie Lab, studying the role of inflammation in initiation and progression of clonal hematopoiesis. Cam completed his PhD in biochemistry at McMaster University in 2025, studying cellular mechanisms of leukemia relapse. Outside the lab, Cam enjoys running and spending time in the outdoors.
